01Cream of Rice

Cream of Rice

Just add water and, in about 30 seconds, you have a smooth staple food that is easy to digest. It works as a training meal for athletes, as an easy everyday meal, as care food (nursing care and similar uses), and as emergency food.

02Video & Events

Video Production & Events

Built on the planning and editing skills we developed running our own media, Anabolic Space (over 100,000 followers, with a peak monthly reach of more than 10 million), we produce video for the fitness world. Message

Even on the days it doesn't go well, get back up and keep going. YAOKI was born from the spirit of nanakorobi yaoki: fall seven times, stand up eight.

Born in 1997 and raised in Australia. After graduating from the University of Sydney's Faculty of Architecture, I joined Fuji Television Network, Inc. in 2020. I worked in program production on the morning show Mezamashi TV, then in the social media countermeasures department of the compliance division. In 2021 I launched the YouTube channel Anabolic Space, and at the end of 2025 I left the company and founded YAOKI Inc.

On the days that go wrong, or when it feels like giving up, get back up and face forward. With the spirit of nanakorobi yaoki in our hearts.
