01Cream of Rice

Rice-based carbohydrates. A new form of staple food.

Cream of Rice

Just add water and, in about 30 seconds, you have a smooth staple food that is easy to digest. It works as a training meal for athletes, as an easy everyday meal, as care food (nursing care and similar uses), and as emergency food.

  • 100% Japanese rice, made in Japan
  • No cooking. Ready in about 30 seconds with water
  • Gluten-free and gentle on digestion

02Video & Events

Fitness-focused creative that carries real intensity.

Video Production & Events

Built on the planning and editing skills we developed running our own media, Anabolic Space (over 100,000 followers, with a peak monthly reach of more than 10 million), we produce video for the fitness world. From filming and editing for IFBB Pro athletes' channels to traveling with athletes to overseas competitions and hosting offline events, we deliver the intensity and the stories of people who take on challenges.

  • Filming and editing for social video
  • Following athletes: on-site coverage at competitions in Japan and abroad
  • Hosting offline events
